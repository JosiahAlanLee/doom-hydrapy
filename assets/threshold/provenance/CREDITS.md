## Originality statement

The floor plan, creature silhouettes and animation frames, wall/flat materials,
props, score and patch plan were generated for this job from the brief. No
existing Doom map was traced; no sprite, texture, sample or tune was copied.
The Argon family direction (Surveyor, Husk, false door, spore spindle) informed
distinctness only; Latchwing and Relay Choir are new designs for this experiment.
The score is original and does not quote or transcribe E1M1 or any other tune.
Descriptions of Quake/Backrooms mood are inspiration, not asset provenance.
